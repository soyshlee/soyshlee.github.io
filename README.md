# Digital Resume Inspired by _The Office_ (U.S. Version)
### Key Idea
To document the website creation process from ideation and planning through production, execution, and publishing.

### Why Make This from Scratch?
Although there are faster and more optimized options to host and design my website, I wanted to challenge myself on creating my own from scratch because 
1. it give me more creative liberty as I won't be limited in color choices or layout designs,
2. it allows me to create an interface that reminds me of pages that were once interactive and fun before everything became commercialized and minimalistic <sub>(Bebo, Myspace, Tumblr circa 2009)</sub>,
3. and it also helps that this is <ins>free</ins> and I wouldn't have to worry about my imagination being stuck behind a paywall.

### Why _The Office_?
This show sparked my love for mockumentaries and was one of the building blocks for my humor as I was very much a #1 fan all throughout high school ~even owning a few "Shrute Farms" t-shirts~.

### Tools Used
- Affinity Designer (Adobe Illustrator Alternative)
- Figma
- Visual Studio Code
- HTML, CSS, and Javascript

### What Was the Vision?
Inspired by a specific <i>bit</i> throughout the series where the character, Jim keeps putting Dwight's office supplies in Jello.

<mark>The most memorable scene is Dwight's stapler in yellow gelatin.</mark>

### Why Did It Fail?
1. Color Palette: The color palette wasn't evoking the feeling of being trapped in gelatin.
2. Too Niche: The reference is only pushed towards the audience the first couple of seasons. After that it becomes a memory therefore making the inspiration for the website only recognizable to the more hardcore The Office fans.
3. Responsive Design: Responsive design was a challenge due to the layout of the website and the specific header I created. It was difficult to keep the vision and make it available on all platforms.

### What I Kept
I kept the corporate atmosphere emphasizing more office supplies such as A4 paper, notepads, and folders rather than just sticky notes.I also made sure to keep the concept of having my links be a part of the theme rather than just links on a navigation bar (i.e. my last design had the links incorporated into  folder tabs).

### What I Did Differently
Rather than a standard header and navigation pane, I decided to focus on involving the user more by humanizing the layout in a way that makes it seem as though the user is looking at a paper on their desk. I also made the theme a bit more playful than before even if it's a little more static than my last design (no jello). In the future, I plan to incorporate audio, night mode, and some easter eggs to make it livelier.

### What I Learned
1. Think of responsive design before coding.
2. Use a combination of Affinity Designer and Figma to brainstorm and create a prototype beforehand.
3. Research color combinations to see if it will mesh well with the story you're trying to portray in your web design.
